Presence and device-transfer fixes

Fixed
- Leaving OrangChat no longer leaves your account showing online indefinitely.
  The app now changes your presence to idle in the background and goes offline
  after five minutes.
- Returning to the app immediately restores your online presence.
- Presence is tracked per device, so an idle phone cannot override a browser or
  desktop app that you are actively using.
- Stale connections now expire automatically if a disconnect is missed.
- Adding a second device no longer requires an authenticator app: accounts
  without two-factor authentication get a one-time email code instead, so
  copying your encryption keys to a new phone or PC can no longer lock you out.

Improved
- The web app changes to idle after being unfocused for ten minutes while
  remaining connected, and returns online when focused again.
- Reading back through a conversation now shows a button to return to the
  newest message, which says "New messages" when something arrived while you
  were scrolled up.
- Replying no longer depends on knowing the swipe gesture: "Reply" is now the
  first entry in the message long-press menu.
- Long-pressing a channel offers "Mark as read", the same action the direct
  message list already had.
