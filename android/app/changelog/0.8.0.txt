Passkeys

New
- Sign in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN instead of a password.
- Accounts that have a passkey are asked for it first - it is phishing-resistant,
  so it beats the emailed code. Email codes remain as a fallback.
- Add, rename, and remove passkeys under Settings > Security.

Fixed
- Passkeys shared with the site and the Android app: a passkey created in the
  browser works in the app and the other way around.
