New
- "Now playing" for games, alongside the existing Spotify line. The desktop app
  recognises around 9,000 titles from the process it sees running and shares the
  one you're in; here on Android you see it on profiles and in member lists, with
  the game's artwork and a clock counting how long you've been playing.
- It is off until you turn it on, in Settings - Privacy - Activity. Detecting a
  game means the desktop app reading the list of programs running on your
  computer, which is not something that should start happening because an update
  shipped.
- Playing something it doesn't recognise? The desktop app's settings let you
  point at a running program and name it yourself. Hand-named games show without
  artwork, so nobody can be tricked into thinking one game is another.

Fixes
- The camera and microphone work again in the desktop app. Calls could not use
  either, with nothing on screen to explain why.
