Hold it, and it's going

New
- Recording a voice message is now press-and-hold, with the gestures the
  idiom comes with: let go to send, slide left to cancel, slide up to lock
  the recording and get your finger back. The release is the send - there
  is no second tap, and no live mic left running when the stop button was
  forgotten.
- The composer shows a live waveform of your voice, the running time, and
  the mic swelling with your volume while it listens.
- Every step has a haptic: starting, locking, cancelling, sending.
- A voice message sends itself the moment its file finishes uploading, with
  a "Sending voice message…" progress row in the composer while it does.
- A recording cancels itself if the app goes to the background or the
  screen locks, instead of keeping the microphone hot.

Fixed
- The swipe-to-delete no longer breaks mid-recording: the mic used to shift
  sideways as the send button vanished under your finger, turning every
  recording into an accidental lock and putting the delete threshold out of
  reach. Nothing in the row moves anymore, and the drag tracks your finger
  directly.
- A too-short press no longer sends a fragment of room tone; it is
  discarded with a brief hint about the gesture.
