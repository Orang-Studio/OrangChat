Bots
- Bot accounts now show a BOT label next to their name in chats and the member
  list. Make your own under Settings -> Developers on the web app.

Fixes
- Sending a message no longer flashes: the greyed-out message you see while it
  sends now turns solid in place instead of disappearing and sliding back in as
  a new one.
- Failing to send used to show a line of internal code instead of what actually
  went wrong. It now says something useful, and the real cause is recorded for
  bug reports.
