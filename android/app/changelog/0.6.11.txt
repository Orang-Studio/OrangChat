Offline cache and video fixes

Fixes
- The offline cache could never be read back after the app was closed, so
  servers, channels and history opened empty and had to be fetched again on
  every launch. Snapshots written before this update are replaced automatically
  the first time each one is refreshed.
- Custom emoji and soundboard clips are kept with the rest of the cache, so
  cached messages no longer show raw :emoji: text before the network answers.
- Opening a channel no longer discards older cached messages that were already
  on screen.
- Losing connection while the app starts no longer signs you out. Only a genuine
  rejection from the server ends a session; captive portals and proxies that
  answer with their own error pages are treated as being offline.

Improved
- Encrypted videos and other large attachments decrypt many times faster. A
  30 MB video that previously took about two minutes now finishes in seconds.
- The attachment progress ring now says whether it is downloading or
  decrypting, instead of appearing to stall at the end of a download.
- Tapping a search result jumps straight to that message, loading the older
  history it sits in if needed, and flashes it so it is easy to spot. If the
  message cannot be reached, the chat now says so instead of silently opening
  at the bottom.
