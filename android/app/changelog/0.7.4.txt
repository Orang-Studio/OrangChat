Rebuilt fullscreen media preview

New
- Fullscreen images and videos now open in a dedicated media screen instead of
  a dialog, giving the preview the phone's real display bounds.

Fixed
- Videos are centered and open with playback controls visible at the bottom.
- Tapping media hides or restores the preview chrome and Android system bars
  together, with no play/pause button covering the middle of the video.
- Returning to chat hands the video surface back to the inline player without
  restarting playback.
- Images stay centered and support pinch, pan, and double-tap zoom.
