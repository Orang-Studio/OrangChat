OrangChat 1.0.2

New
- Mute a conversation or a server from its long-press menu, for 15 minutes,
  an hour, 8 hours, a day, or until you turn it back on. Muted chats stop
  sending notifications and are marked in the direct message list.
- Tap your own status dot in the sidebar to switch between online, idle and
  do not disturb without opening settings.
- Profile cards now show the person's current status on their avatar.

Changed
- Jumping to a message from search or from a reply now lands it near the top
  of the screen instead of the very bottom, so the replies around it are
  visible.
- Message avatars in a conversation no longer carry a status dot; it stays on
  the header, sidebars and member list.
- The idle moon icon is a little fuller and easier to read at small sizes.
