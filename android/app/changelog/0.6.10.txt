Offline access and search

New
- Recent servers, channels, direct messages and message history are now kept in
  an encrypted on-device cache. They open faster and remain readable without a
  connection after you have visited them on this phone.
- Message search is now available in Android for servers, individual channels
  and direct messages. Encrypted conversations are searched privately on this
  device, including when the server cannot be reached.

Privacy
- Offline snapshots are separated by account, encrypted with a key protected by
  Android Keystore, excluded from device backups and erased when you sign out or
  delete your account.

Fixes
- Losing Wi-Fi while opening the app no longer looks like being signed out.
- Fixed encrypted attachment uploads failing after the upload completed because
  Android could not access the generated attachment serializer.
