OrangChat 1.0.4

New
- The emoji picker now leads with a "Recently used" row of your last picks,
  so a favourite emoji or custom server emoji is always one tap away.
- Sending a message no longer waits on its attachment: it goes out right
  away, shows as sending, and completes once the file finishes uploading.
  If the upload fails, the message is marked as not sent so it can be
  retried or discarded.

Fixed
- Custom emoji in the `:` shortcode suggester popup rendered blank; they now
  show their thumbnail like everywhere else in the picker.
- Typed emoji shortcodes were highlighted bold, which nudged the caret out
  of line with the real text; the highlight is colour-only now.
- Opening or closing the emoji/GIF picker no longer bounces the conversation
  above the composer.
- Jumping to a message from search or a reply now lands it precisely at the
  top of the screen instead of a rough estimate.
- The direct message list's last-activity time no longer overlaps the
  avatar; it sits at the end of the title line.
