Fixes
- Videos no longer download themselves just to show a still frame. A clip is
  fetched when you press play and not before, which is what made previews take
  longer the bigger the file was.
- Large attachments (anything over 10 MB) preview and download properly again.
  They were being served as anonymous data, which no player or image loader
  would touch, and a dropped connection restarted the download from zero
  instead of resuming.
- Editing a message that has an attachment can now clear the text completely.
  Save was disabled while the box was empty, so there was no way to remove the
  caption from a photo.
