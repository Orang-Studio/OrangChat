Settings in every language

New
- Every settings screen is now fully translatable - encryption, profile
  themes, ringtone, update checks, the profile card and more. All UI text in
  the app routes through the translation layer.
- Android bundles full catalogues for English, Lithuanian, Mandarin Chinese,
  Hindi, Spanish, Modern Standard Arabic, French, Bengali, Portuguese, Russian
  and Urdu; anything missing is completed by the server on launch and hourly.
- Translations are served by the chat server: a mistranslation can be fixed
  and reach every phone without releasing an APK, and new languages appear in
  the picker on the next poll.

Changed
- The language picker now covers the whole app, not just the launcher.
- String lookups fall back to bundled resources whenever the server is
  unreachable.

Fixed
- Hard-coded English text removed from profile themes, ringtones, voice
  recording and the QR login flow.
