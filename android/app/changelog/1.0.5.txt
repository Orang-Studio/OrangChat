OrangChat 1.0.5

New
- Profile widgets: your profile card is now an ordered list of sections you
  control. Drag to reorder, hide what you do not want, and add more from a
  catalog of widget types - badges, custom text, and more. An empty list
  falls back to the default layout.
- Field tokens: create a revocable token that lets a service you run push
  values onto your profile without your password. Anything it pushes becomes
  available to your widgets as {field.name}, so a status line can show data
  from your own server.
- Channel settings: with the manage-channels permission you can now rename a
  channel, set its topic, user limit, slow mode and age restriction straight
  from the app.
- Channel details: tapping a channel opens its member list, grouped by
  presence.
- Holding a message now opens a bottom sheet with a quick-reaction row on top
  - your recent picks one tap away, a + to open the full searchable emoji
  picker - and the message's other actions below.
- An offline banner appears when the connection drops, so it is clear why
  nothing is arriving.

Improved
- Release builds are now minified and stripped to arm-only, roughly 40% off
  the APK size with no behaviour change.
- Media previews, link embeds and activity status all got small polish
  passes.

Fixed
- Invite links pasted as full https://chat.oranges.lt/invite/... URLs are
  recognised as well as bare codes.
