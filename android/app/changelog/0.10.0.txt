A room can have a face

New
- A conversation can have a background picture, and the chat redraws itself
  to fit: messages sit on rounded, translucent plates that let the picture
  show through the gutters between groups, while system notices stay unplated
  and centred over the scene. The unread divider lands between plates instead
  of splitting one down the middle, and hides while you sit at the bottom of
  the history so an arriving message does not flicker it in and out.
- Group DMs can be given an icon, set by any participant: pick a picture and
  it shows up in the conversation list, the chat header and next to the
  message wherever that group is mentioned. A group that has none shows a
  glyph with the group's first letters instead. The same picture is handed
  to everyone - and to people who join later - so it is plaintext like an
  avatar, never encrypted.
- Messages can be reacted to with an emoji: hover a message and pick from
  the quick set, or open the full picker. The strip under the message shows
  who reacted with what, your own reaction stays on top and toggles, and
  reactions are encrypted end to end with the rest of the message they sit
  on. Reacting works everywhere the message is reachable, including from
  inside the full-screen viewer.
- The full-screen viewer for an image or video now knows what it is showing:
  a bar along the bottom names who sent it, when, whatever caption it came
  with, and carries the reactions so you do not have to close the viewer to
  answer a post. The viewer also grows out of the thumbnail you tapped
  instead of fading in over the whole screen, and pinches to zoom in and out
  like a gallery app.
- An inline video now holds its shape: the box it occupies is picked from
  the clip's own aspect ratio, clamped like a photo, so a portrait phone
  clip no longer towers over the row and a wide letterbox does not render
  as a thin strip. Web and Android agree on the range, so a message looks
  the same on both.

Changed
- Losing every device now has a second, faster way out: a device that still
  holds your keys can erase them on the spot and start over with a fresh
  identity - no waiting period, because signing with the key is the proof.
  The unkeyed path (the email-and-wait erasure) is unchanged.
- The app no longer flags your own account's encryption identity as changed
  right after you erase and re-create it - a pin describing a generation of
  keys that no longer exists was being held against the very person who
  replaced it.

Fixed
- Cards, code blocks and quoted replies inside a group shift up the surface
  ramp onto the plate, instead of disappearing into it when the theme's own
  surfaces match the plate colour.
- A video preview no longer measures itself from the decoder's first frame,
  which could stretch the box while the poster was still loading.
- The chat header and message list keep the same rounding across images and
  video in one row, instead of rounding them differently.
